99 Names does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or contacts, and never sells data. On Android, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which parts of the app are used. All content works fully offline.

About this policy

This policy explains how the 99 Names - Asmaul Husna app for Android ("the app") handles information. The app shows the 99 Names of Allah with their transliteration, English and Urdu meanings and short reflections.

Information we collect

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There is no account and no sign-up.

Anonymous usage statistics (Android only). The Android app uses Google Firebase Analytics to record how the app is used, for example:

  • Which screens are opened, and which Name is opened (by its number, 1 to 99)
  • When a Name is added to or removed from favourites
  • When you tap Share, Rate, one of our other apps, or a link to our website (teamsazen.com) in Settings

Firebase also records standard technical information: app version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country, a random app-instance identifier, and when the app is first opened and used. This is not linked to your name or any account. Collection of the advertising ID is turned off.

Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the app saves a few settings in your device's local app storage:

  • The Names you mark as favourites, stored as their numbers (1 to 99)
  • Your theme choice: System, Light or Dark
  • Whether cards show transliteration, English meaning and Urdu meaning
  • Your Arabic text size

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it permanently.

Internet, permissions and third parties

  • Internet. All content and fonts are built into the app. The Android app connects to the internet only to send the usage statistics above and to check Google Play for app updates.
  • No device permissions. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or any other permission.
  • Firebase Analytics on Android. Usage statistics are processed by Google as described in Google's privacy policy and Firebase's privacy information. The app contains no advertising networks.
  • No data selling. We do not sell data or share it with anyone other than Google as the provider of Firebase Analytics.

Copying text

When you tap the copy button on a Name, its text is placed on your device's clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The app does not read your clipboard.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13; the anonymous Android usage statistics described above contain no name, email address or contact details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/teamsazen/99-names/issues.
