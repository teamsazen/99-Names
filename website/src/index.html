Is Asmaul Husna free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app has no internet access at all. All the Names, meanings, reflections and fonts are built in, so it works anywhere.

What does it collect about me?

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your favourites and settings are saved only on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

In which order are the Names?

In the order narrated by at-Tirmidhi, numbered 1 to 99.

Which languages does it support?

Each Name has its transliteration, an English meaning and an Urdu meaning, shown side by side. You choose which ones appear on the cards.

Is it on iPhone?

Asmaul Husna is on Google Play for Android today.
