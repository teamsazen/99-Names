FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Something else? Get in touch or read the privacy policy.

Is the app free?

Yes. 99 Names is free, with no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no account.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every Name, meaning, reflection and font is inside the app, so it works anywhere, even in flight mode.

What is the Name of the day?

Each day the app opens on one Name, the same for everyone, and goes through all 99 in turn. The card above shows today’s.

What does the app keep about me?

Your favourites and settings stay on your phone. On Android, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which parts are used; it never asks for your name, email or contacts and has no ads. The privacy policy explains the rest.

In which order are the Names?

In the order narrated by at-Tirmidhi, numbered 1 to 99.

How big is the app?

About 2 MB. It is built natively for Android with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, so it downloads in seconds and opens instantly, even on older phones.

Is it open source?

Yes. The code of the app and this website is on GitHub under the MIT licence. Issues and pull requests are welcome.
